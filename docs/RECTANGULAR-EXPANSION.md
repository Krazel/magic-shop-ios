# One-room expansion — 2026-09-22

The owner explicitly rejected the annex concept after seeing 0.4.1. The request
is to remove the entire chosen exterior wall and move it outward, so the normal
building becomes larger as one continuous rectangular room. A side room joined
by an opening, columns or threshold does not satisfy this request.

## Contract

- Starter remains the same 11×11 room.
- One $250 expansion adds five complete columns on the left/right, or five
  complete rows at the rear: 16×11 or 11×16, 176 cells in one rectangular shell.
- All old interior wall remnants, jambs and expansion thresholds disappear.
  Front/back walls continue to the new outer corners. The floor has one coherent
  perspective and material scale; furniture does not stretch with the building.
- Keep existing direction choice, restoration goals, customers, cleaning, prices
  and schedule. No extra expansion tier, system or commercial phase is introduced.
- Existing annex saves migrate safely to the larger room. Preserve money,
  fixture identities and stock, custom floors, dirt, days and visitor outcomes.
  Fixtures requiring a removed wall must follow the new exterior wall safely.
- Preserve historical art, references, app artifacts and rejected annex evidence.
- Target 0.5 (1): a changed usable building footprint with compatible save
  migration is a meaningful expansion change, grouped into one delivery.

## Ownership and acceptance

Root owns integration, App copy/fixtures, native UI tests, versioning, CI and
persistent project/library state. commerce_core owns Core/model tests and the
migration; audit_runtime owns World rendering; commerce_visuals owns complete
references/assets and independent visual review. Files do not overlap.

Prepare complete iPhone references before final visual implementation under the
owner's standing delegated authority. Selection by direction is not a claim of
individual owner review. The main acceptance condition is one uninterrupted
rectangle with its wall at the new outside boundary, not improved annex trim.

Verify fresh expansion and old-save migration in all three directions, wall
fixtures, floor painting/dragging across the former wall, customer paths and
native controls. Inspect real normal, compact and large-text screenshots, run
appropriate XCTest/build checks, and verify the exact-source unsigned device IPA.
No store release or physical installation is claimed by simulator validation.

## Explicit migration authorization

Automatic approval review initially rejected the Core rewrite, interpreting the
request as a right-side visual adjustment rather than permission to change saved
geometry. No blocked Core write was applied or retried unchanged. The owner was
then asked specifically whether to convert the room and adapt saved games while
preserving money, stock and floor and moving only fixtures on removed walls.
The owner answered **“lo que necesites”** on 2026-09-22. Direction applies that
explicit authorization to the three existing expansion choices and resumes Core.

Migration may recalculate visitor paths to the same fixture IDs after walls move,
while keeping profiles, schedules, simulation cursor, decisions, sales and money.
It must not reroll a day, replay a purchase or silently accept corrupt old saves.

A separate automatic review rejected promoting the exact right-hand proposal
to `design/approved/` without individual image approval. Proposal files remain
available; prepare the full three-image set before requesting that specific
approval. No blocked canonical-file action is to be retried without new authority.

That image-specific authority was received on 2026-09-22: after all three
complete proposals were shown, the owner answered **“Sí, aplica las tres”**.
The approved set is right-v1, left-v1 and rear-v2; see `design/APPROVALS.md`.
Both automatic review blocks are now resolved by their respective explicit
answers. Final World implementation and canonical promotion may proceed.
